Our History & Legacy

Rooted in tradition, sustained by family values, and growing strong since 1919.

Our Story

The story of Siganga Family Farm begins in 1919, when our grandfather, **Christopher Siganga**, first broke ground on these fertile pastures. With deep respect for the land and a vision for sustainable agriculture, he established the core principles that guide us to this day.

As the decades passed, the farm was proudly handed down through generations. Each era brought unique changes, but our dedication to raw honey, pure beef tallow, organic traditional greens, and clean pasture-raised livestock has never wavered. Today, we bridge historical wisdom with modern sustainable practices.
